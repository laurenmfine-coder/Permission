Where this fits

The Yes Invoice is step 2 of the Boundary Workup, a clinical method for the automatic yes.

  1. Chief complaint: name what you want room for.
  2. History: log every yes for one week. (This worksheet.)
  3. Presenting complaint: find where the automatic yes happens.
  4. Differential: separate what happened from the fear about saying no.
  5. Verify: test the fear against evidence.
  6. Cost and prescribe: price the yes and choose the words.
  7. Two-week trial: try it, then measure what you got back.
