The published standards

What the guidelines say

The allergy specialty societies publish joint practice parameters on anaphylaxis. The most recent update, Anaphylaxis: A 2023 practice parameter update, appeared in December 2023. It is the most widely cited U.S. anaphylaxis guideline.

Epinephrine is first-line treatment for anaphylaxis, given promptly. Delayed epinephrine has been associated with worse outcomes. Antihistamines and steroids should not delay epinephrine.

The 2020 update advises observing patients after their symptoms resolve, with a longer period after a severe reaction or more than one dose of epinephrine. The 2023 update addresses when a patient treated at home needs emergency care.

Guidelines change. I apply the version in effect at the time of care, and I say which version that is.
