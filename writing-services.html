How it works

Simple. No surprises.

Free Session

A real 25 to 55 minute conversation, not a sales call. You tell me what you're applying for and where you're stuck. I tell you honestly what package fits and what the finished document should do for you.

Common questions

What people ask
before they reach out

Can you help with my medical school application?

No. Due to my faculty role at NSU Dr. Kiran C. Patel College of Allopathic Medicine, I cannot provide personal statement, essay, or application writing help for MD admissions, as this presents a direct conflict of interest. I'm glad to help with residency, fellowship, graduate school (non-MD), or job applications.

Do you work with people across the US and internationally?

Yes. All writing engagements are conducted virtually via video and email. I work with clients across the US and internationally. Location is not a barrier.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the package and your deadline. A Résumé Refresh typically turns around within a week of our working session. The Full Application Package takes longer, since it includes a working session and multiple documents. Tell me your deadline in the free session and I'll tell you honestly whether it's realistic.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your current résumé (even a rough one), the job posting or program description you're applying to, and any draft material you already have. For personal statements, I'll ask you questions in our working session rather than expect a finished draft from you.

Why work with you vs. a template résumé service?

Template services optimize for volume. You fill in a form, and a formula produces a document that sounds like a hundred other applicants. I write a smaller number of documents at a time, starting from an actual conversation about your background, so what comes out sounds like you, not a formula.

What does it cost?

Résumé Refresh starts from $400. Résumé + Cover Letter starts from $650. The Full Application Package (résumé, cover letter, and personal statement) starts from $1,000. Personal statement writing on its own is available too, ask in your free session. Final pricing depends on scope and is confirmed after our first conversation. The first session is always free.

What does the free first session cover?

A real conversation, free, up to 55 minutes. You tell me what you're applying for and where you're stuck. I ask questions, get a feel for your background, and tell you honestly which package fits and what the finished document should do for you. No obligation, no sales pitch.

How many revisions are included?

Résumé Refresh includes one revision round. Résumé + Cover Letter and the Full Application Package include two. If you need more after that, additional rounds can be added.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

Most sessions are conducted via video. In-person sessions are available by appointment in the Weston, Florida area for clients in South Florida who prefer that format.

Where I work

Based in Weston, Florida. Working with clients across the U.S. and internationally.
