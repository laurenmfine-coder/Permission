Are Dryer Sheets Bad for Allergies?

the scent is the point, and the problem

Lauren Fine, MD November 18, 2026 4 min read

Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you buy through one, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Nothing here is sponsored and no brand paid to be included.

Dryer sheets and fabric softener are designed to do one thing: leave something behind on your clothes. Usually that something is fragrance. If you have sensitive skin, eczema or a fragrance allergy, that is exactly what you do not want.

What dryer sheets leave behind

Dryer sheets coat fabric with softening chemicals and fragrance. The coating stays on your clothes, towels and sheets, against your skin all day and all night.

Some of it also goes into the air. In a study of household dryer vents, researchers found more than 25 volatile organic compounds in the exhaust when fragranced detergent and dryer sheets were used, including some classified as hazardous air pollutants.

Who should skip them

  • Anyone with eczema or sensitive skin.
  • Anyone with a known fragrance allergy.
  • Anyone who avoids strong scents.
  • Babies and young children, whose skin is thinner.

What to use instead

  • Wool dryer balls. Reusable and unscented. They help with softness and drying time. Skip the essential oil add-ons if fragrance bothers you.
  • Nothing at all. Clothes come out fine without a softener. Pull them out promptly to reduce wrinkles.
  • A fragrance-free detergent, so you are not adding scent back in the wash. See the best fragrance-free laundry detergent.

What about static?

Static is mostly a synthetic-fabric and dry-air problem. Wool dryer balls help. So does taking synthetics out a little early, before they are bone dry.

Make room

This is one of the easiest swaps in your home. Stop buying the sheets. Toss in dryer balls. Your skin gets one less thing to deal with every day.

Source: Steinemann AC and colleagues, "Chemical emissions from residential dryer vents during use of fragranced laundry products," Air Quality, Atmosphere and Health, 2013.

Frequently asked questions

Are dryer sheets bad for allergies?

They can be. Dryer sheets leave fragrance and softening chemicals on fabric that sits against your skin all day. People with eczema, sensitive skin or a fragrance allergy do better without them.

Can you be allergic to dryer sheets?

Yes. Fragrance is one of the most common causes of allergic skin reactions, and scented dryer sheets leave fragrance on your clothes and bedding.

What can I use instead of dryer sheets?

Wool dryer balls, or nothing at all. Use a fragrance-free detergent so you are not adding scent back in the wash.

Are wool dryer balls better for sensitive skin?

They leave no fragrance or coating on fabric, which makes them a better fit for sensitive skin. Skip scented essential oil add-ons if fragrance bothers you.
