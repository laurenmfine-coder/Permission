The allergist's answerFragrance free, ophthalmologist tested, removed gently.

Eyelid skin is some of the thinnest on your body, so what you put near it matters.

Ignore the word hypoallergenic. It has no legal definition. Here is what it really means. Look for fragrance free and ophthalmologist tested instead, and take mascara off without scrubbing.

Clinique mascaras for sensitive eyes, compared

All three are labeled fragrance free, ophthalmologist tested, allergy tested, and safe for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers. The difference is how they wear and how they come off.

MascaraBest forHow it comes off
High Impact (my pick)Everyday volume and lengthA gentle balm or cleanser
Lash PowerLengthening that resists smudging and flakingWarm water. No remover, no rubbing
High Impact WaterproofHeat, humidity and long daysA balm or eye makeup remover

What to look for

  • Fragrance free. Fragrance is one of the most common causes of allergic skin reactions.
  • Ophthalmologist tested. Tested for use around the eyes.
  • Safe for contact lens wearers, if you wear lenses.
  • Easy to remove. Formulas that need hard scrubbing irritate the lids.

Habits that protect sensitive eyes

  • Replace mascara every three months.
  • Never share it, and never add water to a dry tube.
  • Remove it every night with a gentle balm or cleanser. Do not rub.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best mascara for sensitive eyes?

Look for mascara that is fragrance free and ophthalmologist tested. Clinique High Impact is labeled safe for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.

Is Clinique mascara good for sensitive eyes?

Clinique says its eye makeup is ophthalmologist tested, allergy tested and fragrance free, and safe for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.

What does hypoallergenic mean on mascara?

Hypoallergenic has no legal definition. It is a marketing term. Fragrance free and ophthalmologist tested tell you more.

Is Clinique mascara hypoallergenic?

Clinique labels its mascaras allergy tested, 100% fragrance free and ophthalmologist tested. Hypoallergenic itself has no legal definition, so those three labels tell you more.

Is Clinique mascara ophthalmologist tested?

Yes. Clinique says its mascaras, including High Impact, Lash Power and High Impact Waterproof, are ophthalmologist tested and safe for sensitive eyes.

What mascara is best for contact lens wearers?

Look for a mascara labeled safe for contact lens wearers that does not flake. Clinique High Impact and Lash Power are both labeled safe for contact lens wearers.

Is there a waterproof mascara for sensitive eyes?

Clinique High Impact Waterproof is labeled fragrance free, ophthalmologist tested and safe for sensitive eyes. Lash Power is another option: it resists water and sweat but comes off with warm water.

How often should you replace mascara?

Every three months is a common recommendation. Never share mascara.
