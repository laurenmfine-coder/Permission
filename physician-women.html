Physician Women at a Crossroads

You have thought about leaving more than once this year. Maybe not out loud. It is not a thing you can bring up at work, and the people outside medicine do not quite get why it is complicated.

This is a room where that question is allowed. It is free and it is private. Nothing is for sale inside it.

Request to join the group

It opens on Facebook. I read every request myself.

What it is

  • Physician women who are somewhere between staying and going.
  • Veterinarians, dentists and other women with the degree and the doubt are welcome too.
  • Questions, half-formed plans, and the parts you have not said out loud.

What it is not

  • No coaching pitch. No program. No selling.
  • Not a burnout and resilience group.
  • Not a place where anyone tells you to stay or go.

Lauren Fine, MD is an allergist immunologist and a medical educator. She works with high achievers, in medicine and outside it, who are somewhere between staying and going.
