Frequently asked questions

What is the 4-gift rule?

The 4-gift rule means giving four gifts: something you want, something you need, something to wear and something to read. It started as a way to keep kids’ holidays simple. It works just as well for adults.

What is the best gift for a busy mom?

Something that gives her time, rest or quiet back. A gift that makes one part of her day easier usually lands better than one more thing to manage.

What are good gifts for busy moms in their 40s?

Gifts that protect sleep and recovery, a calmer morning, or an hour that is only hers. Think a sunrise alarm, a massager for sore shoulders, good pajamas and a book she has been meaning to read.

What do you get a mom who has everything?

Skip more stuff. Give an upgrade to something she uses every day, or a book that helps her protect her own time.
