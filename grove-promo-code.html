How to get the most from a first Grove order

  • Stock up on what you run out of. Detergent, dish soap and cleaner reach the free-shipping threshold faster than small extras.
  • Check the cart before you pay. Current discounts and any sale prices show there.
  • Read membership terms. VIP renews automatically. Set a reminder before the year is up if you are not sure you will keep it.
  • Look for Free & Clear. If anyone at home has eczema, asthma or sensitive skin, it is the label that matters most.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Grove promo code?

At the time of writing, code VIP50 takes Grove's VIP membership from $29.99 to $14.99 for the first year. Other first-order discounts show in your cart at checkout.

What is the best Grove deal for new customers?

Grove's offers change through the year. In November 2026 the main new-customer savings are the VIP50 code and first-order subscription discounts. A friend's referral link can also come with a gift set. Check your cart for current offers.

Do I have to subscribe to Grove?

You can shop without a subscription. Grove also offers an optional VIP membership with extra perks. Read the terms before you join, since memberships can renew automatically.

What should I buy on my first Grove order?

Start with what you use most and run out of often: laundry detergent, dish soap and an all-purpose cleaner. Choose Free & Clear versions if anyone at home has sensitive skin or asthma.
