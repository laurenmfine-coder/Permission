Medical Expert Witness / Asthma

Asthma expert witness

I am a board certified allergist and immunologist. Asthma is a core part of my specialty. I review cases in which asthma was missed, undertreated, or poorly managed during an attack, and cases in which an exposure at work or at home is said to have caused or worsened it.

I look at the whole course: the diagnosis, the controller plan, the warning signs, and what happened in the hours before the outcome.

Common questions

Questions these cases often raise

  • Was asthma recognized from the history and findings?
  • Was control assessed and treatment stepped up when it was poor?
  • Were warning signs of a severe attack recognized and acted on?
  • Was the patient discharged with a clear plan and follow-up?
  • Did the claimed exposure more likely than not cause or worsen the symptoms, given the timing and the evidence?

The published standards

The standards I apply

I apply the asthma guidelines in effect at the time of care, such as the NAEPP Expert Panel Report 3 (2007), its 2020 Focused Update, and the GINA reports, and I say which version applies. Standards change, and an opinion should hold the clinician to what was expected then, not now.

In exposure cases, I separate what is known about the allergen or irritant from what the record shows about this patient's timing, symptoms and testing. Exposure levels may need an industrial hygienist.
