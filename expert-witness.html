Why this matters to your case

The question is rarely what the physician knew.

It is whether the physician's thinking was reasonable given what was actually in front of them at that moment. Diagnostic reasoning is not intuition. It is a structured process that is taught, observed, scored and remediated, and a record can be read for evidence of whether it happened.

That process is Dr. Fine's professional field. She directs clinical skills education at a medical school, where she is responsible for how physicians are taught to take a history, build a differential, and revise it. She serves on national committees that develop the examinations used to certify physician competence, which means she works from the same published standards a jury will be asked to apply.

The practical consequence for counsel is this. She can testify not only that a standard was breached, but where in the record the reasoning went wrong and what a reasonable clinician would have done at that point instead. She can do it in plain language, without jargon, in a way a jury can follow. That is a different opinion than most treating specialists are positioned to give, and it applies to cases well outside allergy and immunology.

How an engagement works

What to expect, in order.

Conflicts check and case summary

Send the parties, the jurisdiction, the general allegation and your deadlines. A conflicts check is returned quickly, along with whether the case falls inside her areas of opinion. There is no charge for this step.

Record review and preliminary opinion

Records are reviewed and a preliminary opinion is given verbally before anything is committed to writing. If the record does not support your theory of the case, you will hear that at this stage rather than after a report exists.

Written report

If the case proceeds, a written report is prepared to the standard your jurisdiction requires, with opinions stated to a reasonable degree of medical probability and the basis for each identified in the record.

Deposition and trial testimony

Available for deposition and for trial. Teaching physicians and testifying before a jury draw on the same skill, which is explaining a complex reasoning process to an audience that does not have a medical background.

How she works

Four commitments, stated up front.

The opinion does not depend on who retained her. She accepts engagements from both plaintiff and defense counsel, and the analysis is the same either way. An expert who only ever reaches one conclusion is not an expert.

She will decline cases outside her expertise. A referral to a better qualified colleague is more useful to you than an opinion that will not survive cross examination.

Nothing is written before you have heard it. The preliminary opinion is verbal so that an unfavorable review does not become a discoverable document.

She will tell you what is weak in your case. Including when the record supports the other side. Counsel who learn that early can act on it.
