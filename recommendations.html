I got tired of having a drawer for everything and a system for nothing. So I started keeping fewer things, and only the ones that actually earn a place.

This is that list, in the order I'd actually change it. It starts with the small stuff that makes a home feel calmer, and ends with what I reach for on a Tuesday. Nothing here is about doing more. It's about needing less, and feeling better in what's left.
