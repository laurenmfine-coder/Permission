Frequently asked questions

What is the best book on boundaries?

The most searched is Boundaries by Henry Cloud and John Townsend, a classic written from a Christian perspective. For a practical, secular guide, start with Set Boundaries, Find Peace by Nedra Glover Tawwab.

What is a good book on boundaries with family?

Drama Free by Nedra Glover Tawwab is written for family relationships. For scripts you can use word for word, try The Book of Boundaries by Melissa Urban.

Is there a book on how to say no without feeling guilty?

Yes. When I Say No, I Feel Guilty by Manuel J. Smith is the classic. The Disease to Please by Harriet B. Braiker is written for people pleasers.

What book helps with boundaries at work?

Never Split the Difference by Chris Voss teaches how to say no without saying no. Likeable Badass by Alison Fragale covers being both liked and respected at work.
