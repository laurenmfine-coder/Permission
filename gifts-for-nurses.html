The allergist's noteSkip the scented lotion.

Hand-washing and sanitizer all day are hard on skin. Hand eczema is common in healthcare workers, and fragrance is one of the most common causes of allergic skin reactions.

So the kindest hand cream is the plain one: thick, fragrance free and small enough for a pocket. Many hospitals also ask staff to avoid scented products, so leave the candles and perfumed lotions for someone else. For a boxed gift, Clinique’s fragrance-free holiday sets are an easy pick. Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for a nurse?

Something that makes a twelve-hour shift easier: compression socks, a good water bottle, a fragrance-free hand cream or a massager for sore legs. For night-shift nurses, anything that protects daytime sleep.

What do nurses actually want as a gift?

Comfort on shift and rest after it. Practical gifts that get used every shift land better than decorative ones.

Are scented lotions or candles good gifts for nurses?

Usually not. Many hospitals and clinics ask staff to avoid scented products, and hands that are washed all day do better with a fragrance-free cream.

What is a good gift for a night-shift nurse?

A silk sleep mask, a sound machine or a sunrise alarm. Daytime sleep is the hardest part of nights.

What is a good gift for a nursing student or new grad?

A reliable stethoscope, compression socks and an insulated bottle. They will use all three every shift.
