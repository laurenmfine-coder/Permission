Which one?

Classic aluminum Bialetti pots heat quickly and evenly on gas and electric stoves. They do not work on induction. Stainless steel does.

Either way, the coffee is the same. Pick the one you will be glad to see on the stove every morning.

A ritual, not a routine

The first ten minutes of the day are often the only ten minutes nobody else is asking for.

A routine is something you get through. A ritual is something you return to. The difference is not the coffee. It is whether you let yourself be present for it.

A moka pot cannot be rushed. You fill it, you wait, you listen for it. That small pause is the point. It is a few minutes that belong to you before the day starts belonging to everyone else.

You do not need a perfect morning. You need one part of it that is yours.

How to brew

A moka pot latte in five steps

If you have never used a moka pot, this is all there is to it. The whole thing takes about as long as it takes to warm the milk.

  1. 01

    Fill the base with hot water

    Stop just below the little safety valve. Starting with hot water means the coffee spends less time on the heat and tastes less bitter.

  2. 02

    Add the coffee, but do not press it

    Fill the basket with a medium-fine grind, a little coarser than espresso. Level it with your finger. Do not tamp it down.

  3. 03

    Low to medium heat, lid open

    Screw the top on and set it over gentle heat. Leave the lid up so you can watch the coffee rise.

  4. 04

    Take it off when it starts to sputter

    When the stream turns pale and you hear a gurgle, it is done. Run the base under cold water for a second to stop it from over-extracting.

  5. 05

    Warm the milk and pour

    Froth your milk while the coffee brews. Pour the coffee into a glass, add the milk, and sit down to drink it.

Questions

Good to know

The questions people ask most about a moka pot setup.

Is moka pot coffee the same as espresso?

Not quite. A moka pot brews at much lower pressure than an espresso machine, so it will not make true crema. What it makes is a small, strong, rich coffee that is very close in spirit, and it is wonderful with warm milk.

Will a moka pot work on my induction stove?

Only if it is stainless steel. The Bialetti Venus works on induction. The classic aluminum Moka Express, including the MacKenzie-Childs version, works on gas and electric stoves.

What size moka pot should I get?

Moka pot “cups” are small espresso-sized servings. A three-cup pot makes one generous latte or two small coffees. If you want to share, size up.

Do I need a frother?

No, but it is what makes it feel like a cafe drink. A stand-alone frother is the easiest. If you also want a fast option for busy mornings, the Nespresso Vertuo bundle includes one.

How do I clean a moka pot?

Rinse it with hot water and let it dry fully before you put it back together. Skip soap and the dishwasher for aluminum pots, since they can dull the finish.
